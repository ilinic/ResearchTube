Connect ChatGPT

Add this tunnel to ChatGPT after the connection test succeeds. ResearchTube cannot change your ChatGPT account settings itself.

  1. Copy the Tunnel ID above.
  2. Open ChatGPT Plugins , then select the plus button to create a developer-mode app.
  3. Choose Tunnel under Connection, then select or paste the Tunnel ID.
  4. Name the app ResearchTube, then save it.
